# Show research

These are notes on Trailer Park Boys and its movies, and what each fact changed in the game. Quotes are kept to short catchphrases.

## What made it into the game

| Fact from the show | Where it shows up |
|---|---|
| Ricky's malapropisms, or "Rickyisms": "get two birds stoned at once", "worst case Ontario", "denial and error", "Catch-23 situation", "I'm not a pessimist, I'm an optometrist", "what comes around is all around", "survival of the fitness" | Ricky's taunts and win lines, plus the "FIGHT!" banners |
| Mr. Lahey's "shitisms": shiticane, shit ropes, shit hawks, the winds of shit, shit blizzard, shitquake, shit river, shitageddon, "a shit leopard can't change its spots", "the shit apple doesn't fall far from the shit tree" | Lahey's intros, taunts and win lines, plus the KO banners |
| "Randy, I am the liquor." Season 5, Episode 9, "I Am the Liquor" | Lahey's intro line |
| Ricky's car, the Shitmobile, is a beat-up green 1975 Chrysler New Yorker that once belonged to Julian's grandmother | The car in Ricky's super and the parked car on the park stage are now green (they were wrongly maroon), plus a loading tip |
| Julian always has a rum and Coke in hand and never spills it, even after rolling a car | Julian's taunts and a tip |
| Bubbles repairs shopping carts (about $18 a cart) and lives in a shed with his cats | His select-screen tagline, the Shopping Cart Blitz super and a tip |
| Season 4, Episode 4, "The Green Bastard": Ricky's community day has wrestling in the park, and Bubbles wrestles as the masked Green Bastard "from parts unknown" | New **Community Day Ring** stage, with the Green Bastard cheering from the corner |
| Season 4, Episode 5, "Conky": Bubbles' puppet | Bubbles' taunts, spoken as Conky |
| Season 4, Episode 6: a cougar eats the boys' crop, and Bubbles names it Steve French | One in five Kitty Launchers throws Steve French instead, a big cougar that hits harder and knocks down. He also prowls through the park stage. |
| Randy's nicknames: Bobandy, Cheeseburger Walrus, Lord of the Onion Rings | Randy's tagline and taunts |
| J-Roc says "know what I'm sayin'" ("nomesayin"), raps with T, and sells the boys gear for their schemes | J-Roc's tagline and taunts |
| Cory and Trevor are two hapless fans of Ricky and Julian | They haul a couch across the back of the park stage |
| The boys keep landing in jail. Season 1 opens with Ricky and Julian getting out after 18 months, and *Countdown to Liquor Day* (2009) opens with their release. | New **C Block Yard** stage, with tally marks counting the "days till liquor day" |

New mechanic: **taunt** (`S` or `↓`). Your fighter says one of the lines above and gains 10% meter, but is open to attack until the taunt ends. The CPU sometimes taunts after knocking you down.

## Not used

- "Freedom 35" and the Dirty Burger came up in my memory of the show, but no source I found confirmed them, so they're left out.
- Ricky and Bubbles both say "decent!" in fan lore, but I didn't find a source for it. It stays as a line but isn't presented as a fact.
- Stills or photos from the show aren't used for the fighters. Those are copyrighted frames of real actors. The sprites come from the user's own sheet in `art/reference-sheet.jpg`.

## Movies

- *Trailer Park Boys: The Movie* (2006)
- *Trailer Park Boys: Countdown to Liquor Day* (2009): the boys get out of prison and go back to crime.
- *Trailer Park Boys: Don't Legalize It* (2014): Julian has a scheme in Montreal, legalization threatens Ricky's dope business and sends him to Ottawa, and Bubbles hunts an inheritance while Lahey and Randy chase them.
